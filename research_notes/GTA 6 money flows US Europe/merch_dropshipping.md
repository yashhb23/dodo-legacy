## Key Question 1: Which specific stores/brands are visibly selling GTA 6 merch in the US and EU right now, on which platforms, and are there any revenue estimates?

### Takeaway
The visible market splits into (a) three *official/licensed* channels that all launched 1–14 Oct 2026 (Rockstar Store, Miami Heat "Court Culture" Vice City line, Wynwood Walls "Art of Vice City") and (b) a large, anonymous long tail of unlicensed POD sellers on Etsy (US + UK), Redbubble, TeePublic, Spreadshirt UK, TikTok Shop US, eBay, AliExpress and a cluster of standalone Shopify-style POD sites that falsely call themselves "official" (gtavimerch.store, gtaofficial.store, teesculture.com, ellieshirt.com, merchhunters.com, ioniq-shop.com). No store-level revenue estimate (Shopify tracker, Etsy sales count aggregate, TikTok Shop GMV) for any GTA 6 merch seller surfaced in any source.

### Inferences
- The unofficial market is structurally a long tail of anonymous POD accounts rather than identifiable brands; the only "named" unofficial sellers are standalone domains that impersonate officialness, which is exactly the profile most exposed to trademark enforcement.
- Official supply is now substantial (3 licensed drops in 14 days, 10+ tees, hats, totes, art collab) and priced $30–$55 for tees, which sits *above* typical Etsy/Redbubble POD tee prices ($11–$40) — unofficial sellers compete on price and on designs Rockstar does not make (Jason/Lucia art, neon-sunset posters, neon signs, Hawaiian shirts).
- EU sellers have a geographic arbitrage window: Rockstar does not ship official merch to all of Europe (Hungary, Greece, Ukraine and others excluded per reports), so Etsy UK / Spreadshirt / Amazon EU POD fills the gap in those countries.

## Key Question 4: Evidence of Take-Two/Rockstar takedowns of GTA 6 merch on Etsy, Redbubble, TeePublic, Amazon, Shopify in 2025–2026; what triggers them

### Takeaway
No report of a Take-Two/Ebrand takedown specifically against GTA 6 *merchandise* listings on Etsy, Redbubble, TeePublic, Amazon or Shopify was found. What is documented is that Take-Two's vendor Ebrand runs aggressive, trademark-based dragnets (300 X URLs in Aug–Sep 2026, including journalists and GameStop), that Take-Two asserts the GTA figurative mark and logo with "likelihood of confusion," and that it has litigated marketplace listings (PlayerAuctions) and a sandwich shop ("Vice City Subs"). Triggers evidenced across cases: use of the GTA/VI logo, the words "Grand Theft Auto"/"Vice City" as a brand, and claims of officialness. GTA 6 merch listings remain abundant on Redbubble/Etsy as of ~9 Oct 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Aug 26–Sep 2026: Ebrand, on Take-Two's behalf, filed ~300 DMCA-form notices against X posts/accounts, citing Take-Two's international figurative trademark on Grand Theft Auto and the logo trademark and alleging "likelihood of confusion"; it swept in GameSpot, Grok, GameStop and journalist Stephen Totilo; the claim against Totilo was later dropped; neither Take-Two nor Ebrand commented publicly **[confirmed]** — [Game File](https://www.gamefile.news/p/take-two-gta-ebrand-dmca-notice); [Techdirt, 4 Sep 2026](https://www.techdirt.com/2026/09/04/take-twos-leak-burying-dmca-attempts-snared-gamestop-gaming-journalist-that-did-nothing-wrong/); [Cosmic Book News](https://cosmicbook.news/take-two-gta-6-dmca-journalist)
- Take-Two v. PlayerAuctions (2026): Take-Two's takedowns targeted user-uploaded marketplace item listings; PlayerAuctions argues listings contain no GTA copyrighted material and alleges bad faith under §512(f); pending **[confirmed]** — [Eric Goldman blog, Mar 2026](https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2026/03/do-dmca-takedown-notices-need-to-expressly-refer-to-the-lack-of-fair-use-take-two-v-playerauctions.htm)
