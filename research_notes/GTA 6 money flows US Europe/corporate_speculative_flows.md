## Key Question 1 — Take-Two statements (Q1 FY27, Aug 2026; Oct 2026 updates) and analyst year-one forecasts

### Inferences
- Take-Two's guidance implies roughly $1.3–1.5B of incremental FY27 bookings vs FY26 is attributable to GTA VI in the ~4.5 months after launch (midpoint $8.1B vs ~$6.75B FY26 implied by "20% growth"); this is far below the sell-side unit forecasts × $80–100, which suggests either deliberate conservatism (Zelnick's "pulled-forward demand" caveat) or deferred-revenue accounting on the online component. Treat as inference, not fact.
- The premium-edition skew ($99.99) and Newzoo's $88 assumed ASP mean per-unit consumer spend is ~25–40% higher than GTA V's launch, enlarging the add-on/accessory spend pool around each buyer.
