# GTA 6 digital products and online services money (US + Europe, as of Oct 2026)

Scope note: GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) launches 19 Nov 2026 on PS5/Xbox Series; Standard $79.99, Ultimate $99.99; official pre-orders opened 25 Jun 2026 ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-price/); [GTABoom](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-price-by-country-68d8)). Everything below is about the *unofficial* digital-product and service economy around the launch. GTA roleplay servers and FiveM assets are excluded by assignment. Direct page fetches (WebFetch, Firecrawl) were blocked in this session, so figures come from search-result snippets of the cited pages; where a snippet was the only evidence, it is labelled as such. Status labels: **confirmed** (primary page or two independent outlets), **reported** (single secondary outlet), **unverified** (seller's own claim / rumor).

## KQ2. App stores: top "GTA 6 countdown"/"GTA 6 map" apps, download estimates, monetization, takedowns

### Takeaway
The unofficial GTA 6 app market is small (top Android apps ~10K–26K installs) and already under security-industry scrutiny: BforeAI documented 16 unauthorized GTA 6 apps on Google Play (1 Oct 2026), at least one app was removed from Google Play in June 2026, and Wallpaper Engine countdown wallpapers have been pulled from Steam Workshop for guideline violations. There is no official companion app.

## KQ7. Scams and fakes — what they look like and how much they take

### Takeaway
The dominant GTA 6 scams in 2026 are (a) "VIP early access"/beta-key sites charging ~$250 in crypto only, (b) fake "leaked copy"/demo download sites delivering Vidar infostealer or wallet-drainer approvals, (c) fake pre-order storefronts and PSN/Xbox/Rockstar Social Club phishing, and (d) Android adware apps branded "GTA 6 Beta". No aggregate loss figure has been published; per-victim losses range from $250 fixed fees to full crypto-wallet drains.

### Cited Findings
- **VIP early access (confirmed, Malwarebytes Jun 2026 + PC Gamer/gHacks)**: example site charged $250, accepted only BTC/USDT/ETH; payments irrecoverable; no legitimate early access exists — [Malwarebytes, Jun 2026](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/threat-intel/2026/06/gta-6-early-access-is-nothing-but-a-scam); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/grand-theft-auto-6-vip-early-access-scam-sites-are-already-popping-up-malwarebytes-warns/); [gHacks, 24 Jun 2026](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/06/24/fake-gta-6-vip-early-access-scam-sites-demanding-cryptocurrency-payments/); [LevelUp](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/scammers-offer-fake-gta-vi-early-access-for-hundreds-of-dollars-triggering-expert-warnings/)
