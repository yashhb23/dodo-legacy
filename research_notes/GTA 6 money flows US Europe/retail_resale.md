Scope note: GTA VI launches 19 Nov 2026 on PS5 / Xbox Series X|S; pre-orders opened 25 June 2026 (midnight local); physical "code-in-box" copies ship/pick-up from 12 Nov; digital pre-load from 12 Nov. Standard $79.99 / €79.99 / £69.99; Ultimate $99.99 / €99.99 / £89.99 (Ultimate digital-only at retail in Europe per Rockstar FAQ). Direct page fetches were blocked (DNS failures on resellcalendar.com, levelup.com, kotaku.com), so all claims below rest on search snippets of the cited pages; each is labelled **confirmed** (primary/official or multiple top-tier outlets), **reported** (single reputable outlet or analyst model) or **unverified** (affiliate/aggregator/anecdotal). Data earlier than Sept 2026 is labelled as history where relevant.

## Key Question 1: Pre-order numbers and records; which retailers are winning?

### Takeaway
Take-Two has refused to publish pre-order numbers but called demand "unprecedented and astonishing" (7 Aug 2026 earnings call); the only quantified figures are third-party models (Sensor Tower ~4.38–4.77M units / ~$429–500M by late Aug 2026, 77% PS5, ~89% Ultimate Edition; Newzoo ~$260M). No retailer has published a GTA 6 pre-order record; the clearest "winner" signal is Amazon US and Target repeatedly selling out of boxed code-in-box allocations in October.

### Inferences
- The ~89% Ultimate share means the digital storefronts (Sony/Microsoft) are capturing a disproportionate share of pre-order dollars versus European physical retail, which can only sell the Standard Edition; physical retailers' upside is tied to the lower-margin $79.99/€79.99 SKU plus upgrade codes.
- Amazon/Target sell-outs of code-in-box SKUs in October suggest Rockstar/Take-Two allocated boxed stock conservatively (a code-in-box has no manufacturing constraint beyond printing), i.e. the sell-outs are allocation-driven, not demand-capped.
- A ~5M-unit pre-order base at ~$98 blended ASP implies ~$500M in gross pre-order value before platform cuts, consistent with Sensor Tower's $429–500M range.

### Gaps
- No Take-Two, Rockstar, Newzoo-published or Circana/NielsenIQ pre-order unit chart was found for Oct 2026; Sensor Tower's October update could not be located.
- No retailer (Amazon, GameStop, Best Buy, Walmart, Currys, GAME, Fnac, MediaMarkt) has published a GTA 6 pre-order record statement; no Fnac or GAME-specific coverage surfaced at all.
- No evidence of a GameStop stock-price move tied to GTA 6 pre-orders.

## Key Question 2: Console sales lift attributed to GTA 6 (Circana/NielsenIQ/GfK), PS5 Pro demand, GTA-themed console bundles

## Key Question 4: Independent game shops and code-in-box; European retailer launch campaigns (midnight launches, events)

### Takeaway
Rockstar has confirmed the "physical" edition is a download code in a box with no disc (PS5 codes region-locked, Xbox region-free), and at least two independent retailers (Video Games Plus, Canada; Loot Box Gaming, US) publicly refuse to stock it, while every major chain (Amazon, GameStop, Best Buy, Walmart, Target, GAME, Currys, MediaMarkt) sells it. I found no confirmed in-store midnight launch or launch-event campaign from GAME, Currys, Fnac or MediaMarkt; the launch is digital at midnight local time.

### Cited Findings
**Code-in-box facts (confirmed)**
- Rockstar Support: GTA VI available digitally and physically; "a disc will not be included in the box"; physical copies available for shipping/pickup from 12 Nov — [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions)
