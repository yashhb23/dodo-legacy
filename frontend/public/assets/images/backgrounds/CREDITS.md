# Background textures — AI-generated, abstract only

These three backdrops are AI-generated (higgsfield, nano_banana 2k) and are used
intentionally as **abstract, atmospheric** layers only. None of them depict real
people, real historical events, or real places, so they make no authenticity
claim. All genuine heritage imagery on the site remains real archival material
(see ../history/stack/CREDITS.md).

- `hero-volcanic.webp` — abstract volcanic-basalt + lagoon atmosphere, behind the
  hero. Purpose: evoke the island's geological origin.
- `community-aurora.webp` — abstract aurora ribbons in the four flag colours,
  behind the "Soul of Mauritius" section. Purpose: make pluralism felt.
- `footer-topographic.webp` — abstract topographic contour texture, behind the
  footer. Purpose: a quiet sense of place to close on.
- `stack-calm.webp` — very dark, serene indigo field behind the 3D historical
  timeline. Purpose: a calm base so the photo cards feel restful.
- `timeline-modal-calm.webp` — soft dark teal/navy behind the "Island Story"
  timeline modal. Purpose: keep that modal serene and on-brand.

All are applied at low opacity behind a dark theme, so they read as texture, not
as photography. To remove or swap one, edit the `backgroundImage` in the relevant
component (HeroSection, CommunitySection, Footer).
