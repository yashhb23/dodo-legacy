# GTA 6 in Mauritius: Selling Products Locally and Running a Licence Pass-Through (16 Oct – 19 Nov 2026)

Prepared 16 October 2026 from web research on that date. Labels: **[C]** confirmed by an official or primary source, **[R]** reported by press or trade sources, **[U]** unverified. Not legal or tax advice; confirm the flagged items with the MRA, CBRD and the suppliers named.

---

## 0. Straight answer on the "pass-through from Rockstar" idea

**You cannot buy GTA 6 licences from Rockstar and pass them to Mauritian customers.** Four verified reasons:

1. **Rockstar has no reseller, affiliate or partner programme.** Its games reach customers only through the PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, the Rockstar Store, and ordinary retail distribution. **[C]**
2. **Mauritius has no PlayStation Store or Xbox Store.** The only African country on either list is South Africa. Mauritian players already run US, UK or France accounts. **[C/R]**
3. **PS5 download codes are region-locked** to the account's store region. Rockstar's own support page says the code "works differently depending on platform and country of purchase" and tells buyers to purchase from a retailer in the country of their platform account. Xbox codes are reported to be region-free for GTA 6, but Microsoft's general code terms are region-restricted, so treat that as **[U]** until tested. **[C/R]**
4. **The Rockstar Store does not ship to Mauritius** (about 30 regions served; South Africa is the nearest). Whether its console checkout hands over a PSN or Xbox code or just links out to Sony and Microsoft could not be verified. **[C/U]**

**What a legitimate pass-through actually looks like in Mauritius:** you sell the customer the *means* to buy GTA 6 on their own account, at the right region, paid in rupees via Juice, Blink, my.t money or card, and you never touch their account. Three compliant forms exist, ranked below. This is exactly what the existing local players already do, so the market is proven; your job is to do it faster, cleaner and with better bundles.

## 1. The three legitimate pass-through models

| Model | How it works | Margin | Risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Region-matched gift cards** (US / UK / France PSN and Xbox) | Customer tells you their account region and edition. You sell them the exact card value ($80 or $100; £70 or £90; €80 or €100), paid in MUR. They redeem and buy GTA 6 themselves at list price. | 3 to 10 % on the card, plus your service fee | Low to moderate. The only real risk is sourcing from an unauthorised wholesaler whose cards get revoked. | **Do this first.** Instant delivery by WhatsApp, zero stock, zero customs. |
| **B. Code-in-box Standard Edition imports** (UK or France boxes) | The physical edition is a download code in a case, Standard Edition only, shipping from 12 Nov. Import UK boxes for UK-account customers and FR boxes for FR-account customers. | Thin. UK RRP £69.99; local AAA retail is Rs 2,750 to 4,250 and a local shop is already pre-selling from 12 Nov. | Moderate: region mismatch if you sell the wrong box, retailer allocation limits, 15 % VAT plus fees on import, timing. | Offer it for customers who want a box to hold or gift, priced as a premium. Do not build the business on it. |
| **C. Buying-agent service** | Customer pays you in MUR; you buy and deliver the card or code and provide redemption help. | Flat fee (Rs 150 to 300) on top of A or B | Low, **only if you never log into a customer's account.** Handling credentials breaks Sony and Microsoft terms and makes you liable for the account. | Bundle this into A as "we set you up". |

**Never do these:** sell "shared accounts" (a local site is doing it at Rs 3,850; it breaks Sony's terms and the buyer loses access when the account is locked), use Turkey or Argentina pricing tricks (Sony closed the Turkey loophole in Sept 2024 and bans accounts), source cards from marketplaces that cannot verify provenance, or log into customer accounts.

### 1.1 Where to source cards legitimately
- **Open-registration B2B platforms:** CodesWholesale (API, open registration, 400+ distributors) **[R]**; Nexway reseller portal (terms on request) **[R]**. Expect: registered company, tax ID, KYC, prepaid deposit, API or portal ordering.
- **Closed channels to approach by email:** Startselect (authorised reseller of PSN and Xbox cards; no public B2B signup found), Genba Digital (publisher-controlled, invitation only), Blackhawk Network, InComm, Epay/Euronet, Coda Payments **[U]** for each as a Mauritius supplier. Ask each for a reseller account; one yes is enough.
- **Sony Middle East & Africa FZE (Dubai)** covers PlayStation for 40+ MEA countries. Ask them who distributes PlayStation software and cards for the Indian Ocean. **[R]**
- **Eneba has no wholesale option; Kinguin cannot verify codes.** Do not use them as supply. **[C/R]**
- Realistic wholesale discount: 5 to 7 % on US cards, 6 to 9 % India, 8 to 12 % Turkey. Anyone promising 15 to 20 % is selling grey stock. **[R]**

### 1.2 Where to source code-in-box units
- Amazon UK, Currys, GAME (UK); Fnac, E.Leclerc, Micromania (France); Nexus Hub and Takealot (South Africa, but SA codes need SA accounts, which few Mauritians use). Boxes ship 12 Nov, so order the moment pre-orders allow and use air courier. **[C/R]**
- Ask UK and French distributors for a trade account later; for November, retail plus forwarder is the only route that arrives in time.

## 5. 34-day execution plan

**Week 1 (16 to 22 Oct): legal, payments, supply**
1. Register the BRN. Open a business bank account. Apply to MIPS or Peach Payments.
2. Email CodesWholesale, Nexway, Startselect, Genba and Sony MEA the same day asking for a reseller account for PSN and Xbox cards (US, UK, FR). Register on CodesWholesale now since it is open.
3. Order the first accessory batch by air (docks, skins, grips, LED strips) so it lands by early November. Get an MRA tariff ruling by email on the HS codes.
4. Place deposits with a South African or UAE retailer for GTA VI DualSense pads.
5. Brand: a name with no Rockstar marks. Generate logo, banner, product cards and neon décor art with Higgsfield.

**Week 2 (23 to 29 Oct): pre-orders open**
6. Launch the WhatsApp catalogue and one-page site. Products: GTA 6 Standard/Ultimate (digital, by region), DualSense pads (pre-order, deposit), launch kits, décor.
7. Take deposits via Juice. Publish a short "which account region should I use" explainer (US recommended for price, FR for French speakers) and your setup service.
8. Pre-order UK and FR code-in-box units from Amazon UK and Fnac for the customers who want boxes.

**Week 3 (30 Oct to 5 Nov): stock and content**
9. Receive accessories; assemble kits; shoot product photos (Higgsfield can clean and stage them).
10. Print the first run of unbranded tees and posters locally (MOQ 30).
11. Confirm the launch-night venue and a small sponsor (energy drinks, a local telco) to cover the room.

**Week 4 (6 to 12 Nov): pre-load week**
12. Deliver gift cards to every pre-paid customer before 12 Nov so they can pre-load. This is your biggest trust moment: deliver early, publicly.
13. Boxes ship from retailers on 12 Nov; track and clear customs with your broker.

**Launch (19 Nov) and the following month**
14. Midnight launch: redemption help desk, pads and kits on sale, décor display.
15. Days 1 to 30: PS Plus and Game Pass cards, second accessory batch, Ultimate upgrade cards (a roughly $20 upgrade is reported), DualSense restocks, Christmas bundles. Online mode is expected in 2027 **[R]**, which is your next spike.

## 6. Money expectations (honest)

- A card-based GTA 6 sale earns roughly 3 to 10 % plus a Rs 150 to 300 service fee: about Rs 300 to 600 per customer. 100 pre-orders is Rs 30k to 60k. The game itself is a traffic product.
- The margin is in accessories (2 to 4× on kits), DualSense pads (Rs 1,500 to 2,500 each if you secure allocation), décor and tees (Rs 300 to 1,500 each), and recurring card sales.
- A realistic first-month target for one person executing this hard: 100 to 200 game customers, 50 to 100 kits, 20 to 40 pads, a few dozen décor pieces. That is low six figures in rupees of revenue with a healthy share as margin, before tax.

## 7. Open items to verify before you commit money

- Rockstar Store console checkout behaviour (does it hand over codes, and for which regions). Rockstar does not ship to Mauritius either way.
- Whether GTA 6 Xbox codes are truly region-free (reported, not confirmed by Microsoft).
- Onboarding terms and Mauritius eligibility at CodesWholesale, Nexway, Startselect, Genba, Blackhawk, Epay.
- Sony MEA's Indian Ocean distributor for PlayStation software and cards.
- Exact duty rates for HS 9504.50, 6109 and 4911 from the MRA.
- Whether Rockstar's and Take-Two's marks are registered in Mauritius (affects the parallel-import exposure on official merch).
- Status of the Fair Trading (E-Commerce) Regulations 2025.
